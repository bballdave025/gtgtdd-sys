# Principles

## 1. Good-enough trying beats perfect inaction.
## 2. Foundation before cathedral.
## 3. Capture first, organize second, refine third.
## 4. DISCUSS is valid, but ideas must be promoted to become structural.
## 5. New modes require:
- one day of incubation
- a devil's-advocate pass
- a normal follow-up discussion before adoption
## 6. Search before build.
## 7. Keep the system usable on bad days.
## 8. Separate foundational items from someday/maybe items.
## 9. Q&R has a place, but should be clearly labeled when used.
